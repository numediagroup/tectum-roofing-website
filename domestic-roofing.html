Felt Roofing

Felt roofing is a reliable, cost-effective roofing solution widely used on flat and low-pitched roofs for both domestic and commercial properties. At Tectum Roofing, we install and repair high-performance felt roofing systems designed to provide long-lasting weather protection.

  • Our felt roofs are installed using modern torch-on and multi-layer techniques, offering improved durability compared to traditional felt.
  • Ideal for garages, extensions, porches, sheds, and commercial buildings.
  • Provides excellent waterproofing and resistance to the UK climate, including heavy rain and temperature fluctuations.
  • We carefully prepare roof surfaces to ensure proper adhesion and longevity.
  • Available in different finishes to suit the appearance and functional needs of your property.
  • Felt roofing offers a strong balance between affordability and performance.
  • Our team carries out new installations, repairs, and full replacements.
  • All work is completed in line with current building regulations and safety standards.
  • Fully insured and installed by qualified, experienced roofers.
  • Suitable for both planned upgrades and emergency repairs.
  • We provide honest advice on whether felt roofing is the right option for your roof.
  • Serving clients across London, Surrey, Sussex, and surrounding areas.
  • Designed to deliver peace of mind and long-term protection.

GRP (Fibreglass Roofing)

GRP roofing, also known as fibreglass roofing, is a modern flat roofing solution with exceptional durability.

Get a Quote
  • Ideal for flat roofs, balconies, terraces, and extensions.
  • Creates a seamless, fully waterproof surface.
  • Extremely resistant to cracking, standing water, and UV damage.
  • GRP roofs can last 30+ years with minimal maintenance.
  • Installed in one continuous layer, reducing the risk of leaks.
  • Provides a clean, modern finish suitable for contemporary properties.
  • Low maintenance compared to traditional flat roofing systems.
  • Suitable for both residential and commercial buildings.
  • Our team uses high-quality GRP materials for long-term performance.
  • Installation is carried out by fully trained specialists.
  • Can be finished in a range of colours and textures.
  • Ideal for roofs with complex shapes or detailing.
  • Fully insured and compliant with building standards.
  • Available across London, Surrey, Sussex, and nearby areas.
