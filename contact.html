About Us

A Name You Can Trust

At Tectum Roofing, we bring over 15 years of hands-on experience delivering high-quality roofing solutions for domestic and commercial clients across London, Surrey, Sussex, and surrounding areas. Our reputation has been built on reliability, skilled workmanship, and a commitment to long-lasting results.

We specialise in a full range of roofing services, from roof repairs and maintenance to complete roof installations for both residential properties and commercial buildings. No matter the size or complexity of the project, our team approaches every job with the same attention to detail and professional standards.

Tectum Roofing is fully insured and fully qualified, holding all necessary industry accreditations to give our customers complete peace of mind. We work in line with current UK building regulations and best practices, ensuring every roof we install or repair is safe, durable, and built to withstand the British climate.

Customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do. We pride ourselves on honest advice, clear communication, and dependable service from the initial inspection through to project completion. Whether you’re a homeowner in need of trusted roof repairs or a business looking for a reliable commercial roofing contractor, Tectum Roofing is a name you can trust.

Contact Tectum Roofing today for professional roofing services across London, Surrey, Sussex, and beyond.
