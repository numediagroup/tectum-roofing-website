About Tectum Roofing

Roofing Professionals in South London and the Surrounding Areas

At Tectum Roofing, we bring over 15 years of hands-on experience delivering high-quality roofing solutions for domestic and commercial clients across London, Surrey, Sussex, and surrounding areas. Our reputation is built on reliable service, skilled workmanship, and long-lasting results. We offer a full range of roofing services, from repairs and maintenance to complete roof installations, with every project completed to high professional standards.

Fully insured and qualified, Tectum Roofing holds all relevant industry accreditations and works in line with current UK building regulations to ensure every roof is safe, durable, and built to withstand the British climate. Customer satisfaction is at the heart of what we do, with honest advice, clear communication, and dependable service from start to finish. Whether you need trusted roof repairs or a reliable commercial roofing contractor, Tectum Roofing is a name you can trust.
